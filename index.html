About Pheonix Home Services

Honest work. Useful appliances. Less waste.

At Pheonix Home Services, we believe a good appliance should not be thrown away just because it needs attention. We repair appliances, restore used units, sell dependable replacements, and haul away unwanted equipment throughout the Fort Myers area.

Our goal is simple: provide professional service, help customers get more value from their appliances, and keep reusable equipment out of the waste stream whenever possible.

What customers can expect

  • Clear communication
  • Professional appearance and service
  • Practical repair recommendations
  • Quality-focused used appliance options
  • Respect for your home and time
