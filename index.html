About Pheonix Home Services

Quality appliances deserve a second life.

Pheonix Home Services is a local appliance business focused on straightforward repairs, dependable used appliances, and responsible hauling. The goal is simple: help people get more life out of the appliances they already own and keep usable equipment out of the landfill.

Every job is approached with practical recommendations, clear communication, and respect for your home. Appliances offered for sale are inspected, tested, and cleaned before they are made available.

  • Clear, honest communication
  • Practical repair recommendations
  • Used appliances prepared for resale
  • Local service around Fort Myers
